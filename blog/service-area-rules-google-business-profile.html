The Service-Area Rules That Get Contractors Suspended.

Most contractor suspensions trace back to five rules for businesses that drive to the customer.

Published November 12, 2026

You're probably a service-area business

If you drive to the customer and customers don't come to you, Google treats you as a service-area business. That comes with its own rules, and breaking them is one of the most common reasons contractor profiles get suspended.

If you also have a shop or showroom with staff that customers visit during business hours, you're a hybrid business: you can show that address and set a service area.

Rule 1: Hide your address

Google says a service-area business should hide its address from customers. If customers don't come to you, remove the address from your profile and show only your service area.

Your address still matters to Google behind the scenes. It has to be a real place where the business operates, and it has to match your documents if you ever appeal.

Rule 2: No PO boxes or virtual offices

Google says PO boxes aren't acceptable and a virtual office isn't eligible for a Business Profile. A mailbox store address, a coworking desk you never use, or a relative's house you don't work from all create risk. Use the address where the business actually runs.

Rule 3: Keep the service area within about 2 hours

About 2 hours' drive

Google says the overall service area shouldn't extend farther than about 2 hours of driving from where you're based.

Up to 20 areas

You can list up to 20 service areas, by city, ZIP code or another area type.

No radius

You can't set a radius around your shop. Pick the cities or ZIP codes you actually serve.

Changes take time

Google says approved edits may take up to 48 hours to show.

Source: Manage your service areas.

Listing every suburb in two states to show up in more searches is the kind of edit that triggers a fresh review of the profile.

Rule 4: One profile per business

Google allows a service-area business that travels to customers one Business Profile. A second profile for another town, a second phone number or a "new" business name while the first is suspended breaks the rules and makes reinstatement harder.

Rule 5: Use your real business name

Google says the name should be the name you use in the real world, on your truck, your invoices and your license. Adding extra words "could result in the suspension of your Business Profile."

Not allowed: "Smith Plumbing - Best Emergency Plumber Kansas City" when your license says "Smith Plumbing".
Not allowed: a phone number, a tagline or a city name added to the name.
LLC, Inc. and similar terms need real-world proof that they're part of the name.

Source: Guidelines for representing your business on Google.

Quick self-check

Customers don't visit you, and your address is hidden.
Your address is a real place the business runs from, not a PO box or virtual office.
Your service area is 20 areas or fewer, all within about 2 hours' drive.
You have one profile for the business.
Your profile name matches your license and your truck, word for word.
