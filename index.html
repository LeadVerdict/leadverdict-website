What we fix

Three ways a Google listing breaks

Suspended or missing

Profile pulled from Maps

Usually triggered by a shown home address, a service area that's too wide, a name with keywords in it, or a recent move. We find the cause before you appeal.

What causes suspensions
Fake reviews

One-star attack

A burst of star-only one-stars from accounts with one review is a pattern Google's policy names. We write each report with the exact policy reason to pick.

Wiped or paused

Real reviews gone

Google's spam filter removes real reviews by mistake and can pause new ones. We draft the request for a second look, with the details Google asks for.

How a case works

You keep the keys

  1. Send the listing

    Your profile link, the Google notice or the reviews in question, and what changed recently.

  2. We build the case file

    Each problem is matched to Google's own policy. You get the appeal text, the document checklist (license, registration, utility bill, all matching your listing exactly) and any review reports.

  3. You file it

    From your own Google account. Google gives you one appeal, then one request for additional review, so the first filing has to be right.

What we never do

The other offers in your inbox do these

  • Ask for your Google login. You file everything yourself.
  • Promise a review comes down or a profile comes back. Google decides, and anyone who guarantees it is guessing.
  • Write, buy or filter reviews. The FTC fines that by the violation.
  • Report honest reviews from real customers. Those stay up.
