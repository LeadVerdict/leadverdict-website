LeadVerdict is a real estate referral service. We connect homeowners and homebuyers with licensed real estate agents in their local area. We are not a real estate brokerage and do not represent buyers or sellers directly in transactions.

We collect information in two ways: information you give us directly, and information collected automatically when you use the Site.

Information you provide

When you submit a form on our Site, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • The address of the property you wish to sell or are interested in buying
  • Information about your timeline, the property's condition, and your asking price (if applicable)
  • Any additional details you choose to share with us

Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, we (and the analytics services we use) may automatically collect:

  • Your IP address and approximate geographic location

04Why We Collect It

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To match you with an agent. Your property details and contact information are used to identify and connect you with an appropriate licensed real estate agent in your area.
  • To respond to your enquiries. If you contact us, we use your details to reply.
  • To improve our service. We use anonymized usage data to understand how visitors interact with the Site and to improve our matching process.
  • To comply with the law. Where required, we may use or retain information to meet legal obligations.

We do not use your information to send unsolicited marketing material from third parties.

We share your information only with the following:

  • The licensed real estate agent we match you with. This is the entire purpose of the service.
  • Service providers who help us operate the Site. This includes our hosting provider, our form processor (Formspree), our automation platform (Make.com), and our customer relationship tools (Google Workspace).
  • Legal authorities, when legally required. If we receive a valid legal request (such as a court order or subpoena), we may disclose information as required.

What we will never do: Sell your personal information to data brokers or advertisers. Share your information with anyone other than the agent matched to your enquiry. Send your details to multiple agents without your consent.

These include essential cookies needed for the form to function and analytics cookies that help us understand which pages are most useful. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

07Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access. You can ask what information we hold about you.
  • Correction. You can ask us to fix incorrect information.
  • Deletion. You can ask us to delete your information from our records.
  • Objection. You can object to certain uses of your information.
  • Portability. You can ask for a copy of your information in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at leadverdict.co@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act gives you specific rights. We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration.

European residents (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation. Our legal basis for processing your information is your consent and our legitimate interest in operating our matching service.

08How We Protect Your Data

We take reasonable precautions to keep your information safe. We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS) for all data transmitted through the Site, our service providers are vetted and contractually required to protect your data, and access to your information is limited to people who need it to do their jobs. No system is 100% secure, but we work to use the protections that are reasonable for a business of our size and nature.

We keep your information for as long as we have a reasonable business need — typically while we are working to match you with an agent and for a period afterward. If you ask us to delete your information, we will do so promptly, except where we are required to keep certain records by law.
