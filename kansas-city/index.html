What to Look for in a Kansas City Real Estate Agent

Not all agents are created equal. When evaluating a KC agent, look for these specifics:

  • Recent closed volume: At least 15-20 transactions in the past 12 months in KC
  • Days on market average: Below 20 days means they price and market effectively
  • List-to-sale ratio: 98%+ means homes sell close to (or above) asking price
  • Neighborhood specialization: An agent selling in OP won't know Independence comps
  • Marketing plan: Professional photos, 3D tours, and a social/digital strategy — not just MLS
  • Communication style: Weekly updates minimum. Your agent should respond same-day
