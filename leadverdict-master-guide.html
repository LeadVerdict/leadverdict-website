§ Section I

Site Architecture · i

The LeadVerdict website is a single-page composition with five primary movements, designed to function as both a marketing site and a quiet positioning statement. The user journey is deliberately linear — each section answers a question raised by the previous.

SectionPurposeAnchor
HeroEstablish positioning & trust#top
ApproachThree-pillar methodology#approach
PortfolioRecent engagements grid#properties
AdvisorsNetwork bios (asymmetric grid)#advisors
JournalEditorial articles & thought leadership#journal
ConsultationLead capture form#consult

The page is built as a single HTML file with Tailwind CSS via CDN. No build step required. The entire site weighs under 90 KB before images.

§ Section II

Design Philosophy · ii

The aesthetic direction is Editorial Luxury — drawing from the visual language of Architectural Digest, the typography of legacy magazines, and the restraint of private wealth advisory firms. The site does not look like a real estate site. That is intentional.

Five Design Tenets

  • Restraint over abundance. Generous negative space communicates exclusivity.
  • Serif over sans for headlines. Authority comes from typographic tradition.
  • Brass over gold. Aged metal accents, never bright or saturated.
  • Asymmetry over symmetry. Editorial layouts feel composed, not generated.
  • Reveal over disclose. Information emerges as the visitor scrolls.

If a page element looks like a "real estate website" element, redesign it to look like a magazine layout instead.

§ Section IV

Typography · iv

Display — Cormorant Garamond

Used for all headlines, property names, and editorial pull quotes. The italic variant (weight 300) is reserved for emphasis and key phrasing — never used for entire paragraphs.

font-family: 'Cormorant Garamond', Georgia, serif;
weights: 300 (italic), 400 (regular), 500 (semibold)

Body — Inter Tight

Used for all running text, navigation, form labels, and metadata. Default body weight is 300 (light) — heavier weights only for buttons and emphasis.

font-family: 'Inter Tight', -apple-system, sans-serif;
weights: 300 (default body), 400, 500 (UI & buttons)

Mono — JetBrains Mono

Used only for metadata labels, dates, and technical content.

§ Section V

Spacing & Layout · v

The site uses a 12-column max-width container of 1280px with generous internal spacing. Vertical section padding is intentionally large to create breathing room.

  • Container max width: max-w-7xl (1280px)
  • Container horizontal padding: px-6 lg:px-12 (24/48px)
  • Section vertical padding: py-32 lg:py-48 (128/192px)
  • Card internal padding: p-8 lg:p-12 (32/48px)

Asymmetric Grid Pattern

Used in the Approach and Advisors sections. Headlines sit in col-span-9 col-start-4, with section labels in col-span-3. Advisor cards alternate vertical positions using mt-32 to create editorial rhythm.

§ Section VI

Component Catalog · vi

Buttons

Primary (.btn-primary) — solid brass, used for main CTAs only. Maximum two per page.

Ghost (.btn-ghost) — transparent with brass border, used for secondary actions.

Property Card

Used in the portfolio section. Image area is 4:3 aspect ratio with placeholder pattern. Replace inner placeholder div with <img> tag for live deployment.

Advisor Portrait

Used in the advisors section. Portrait area is 3:4 aspect ratio. Replace inner placeholder div with <img> tag for live deployment.

Form Field

Underline-only style (no boxes). Border-bottom is brass at 30% opacity, intensifying to full brass on focus.

Brass Rule (.brass-rule)

Decorative horizontal divider. Linear gradient that fades from transparent to brass at 25% to transparent. Used between sections.

§ Section VII

Tone of Voice · vii

Voice Pillars

  • Considered — never reactive, urgent, or salesy
  • Discreet — implies access without bragging
  • Authoritative — confident without bravado
  • Editorial — reads like a magazine, not a brochure

Vocabulary

Prefer

  • "Engagement" instead of "deal"
  • "Advisor" instead of "agent" (where possible)
  • "Principal" instead of "client" (where appropriate)
  • "Consultation" instead of "consultation call"
  • "Portfolio" instead of "listings"
  • "Considered" instead of "thoughtful"
  • "Network" instead of "team"

Avoid Always

  • Exclamation points
  • "Awesome", "amazing", "incredible"
  • "Don't miss out", "act now", "limited time"
  • Emoji in any public-facing copy
  • "Hustle", "grind", "deal of a lifetime"
  • "Best", "top", "#1"

If a sentence could appear in a Sotheby's catalog or a private bank brochure, it belongs on LeadVerdict. If it could appear in a Zillow ad, it does not.

§ Section VIII

Imagery Standards · viii

The site currently ships with placeholder elements where photography belongs. When replacing placeholders with real imagery, follow these standards:

Property Photography

  • Minimum resolution: 1600 × 1200 (4:3 aspect)
  • Style: editorial — exterior establishing shot or signature interior
  • Lighting: natural, never overexposed
  • No HDR oversaturation, no fish-eye lens
  • Ideal sources: Unsplash (curated), AD listings, professional architectural photographers

Advisor Portraits

  • Minimum resolution: 900 × 1200 (3:4 aspect)
  • Style: editorial portrait, neutral background, considered expression
  • No corporate headshot poses (crossed arms, big smile, business card stance)
  • Subject in middle to upper third of frame
  • Black and white acceptable — even preferred for consistency

How to Replace a Placeholder

Find any element with class property-image-placeholder or agent-portrait-placeholder in the HTML. Replace the <div> with this:

<img src="path/to/image.jpg"
     alt="Descriptive alt text"
     class="absolute inset-0 w-full h-full object-cover">

§ Section IX

Deployment · ix

Recommended: Netlify (free)

  1. Sign up at netlify.com (free, no credit card)
  2. Drag leadverdict-website.html onto the dashboard
  3. Site goes live instantly at a random URL
  4. Site Settings → Change site name → leadverdict
  5. Final URL: leadverdict.netlify.app
  6. Optional: connect custom domain for ~$12/year

Pre-Deployment Checklist

  • Form action URL replaced with real Formspree endpoint
  • Tidio chatbot widget script added (if using)
  • Property placeholder elements replaced with real images
  • Advisor placeholder elements replaced with portraits
  • Footer email address verified
  • Open Graph meta tags updated with final URL
  • Tested in Chrome, Safari, and mobile (responsive design verified)

Formspree Setup (free form handling)

  1. Sign up at formspree.io (free tier: 50 submissions/month)
  2. Create a new form, name it "LeadVerdict Consultation"
  3. Copy the form endpoint URL
  4. In leadverdict-website.html, find YOUR_FORMSPREE_ID
  5. Replace with your real endpoint

§ Section X

Maintenance · x

Adding a New Property

Find the Portfolio section in the HTML. Copy any existing <article class="property-card"> block. Update the placeholder letter, title, location, days, and price. Replace the placeholder div with an <img> tag.

Adding a New Advisor

Find the Advisors section. Copy any existing <div class="lg:col-span-5"> block. Update name, title, year, and bio. To preserve the asymmetric layout, alternate col-start-1 and col-start-8 mt-32 classes.

Adding a Journal Article

Find the Journal section. Copy any existing <article> block. Update category label, date, headline, and excerpt. Wrap the entire article in an <a href="article-url.html"> when ready to link to full posts.

Updating the Stats Bar

The stats in the hero section are hard-coded in the HTML. Update them monthly with real numbers as the business grows. The current values are reasonable for a launching firm — adjust upward as appropriate.
