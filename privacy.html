Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

LeadVerdict ("we," "us," or "our") helps HVAC and plumbing contractors with websites, SEO and Google Business Profiles. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit leadverdict.vercel.app or contact us, what we do with it, and the choices you have.

1. Information you give us

When you fill out a form on our site, we collect what you type into it:

  • Free marketing audit: your name, email, website address and, if you add it, your phone number.
  • Google Business Profile case review: your name, business name, email, optional phone number, your Google profile link, and anything you tell us about what happened.
  • Newsletter: your email address.
  • Email: anything you send to leadverdict.co@gmail.com.

We never ask for your Google account password, and you should never send it to us.

2. Information collected automatically

When you visit the site, these tools collect standard usage data such as your IP address, browser, device, the pages you view and how you got here:

  • Google Analytics, to see which pages people use. Opt out of Google Analytics.
  • Meta Pixel, to measure whether our Facebook and Instagram ads lead to visits and form submissions. Meta may use this data under its own policy, and you can manage it in your Meta ad settings.
  • Vercel, our web host, which keeps short-term server logs to run and secure the site.
  • Google Fonts, which delivers the site's fonts and sees your IP address when it does.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works without them.

3. How we use it

  • To answer your request: send your audit, review your Google listing, or reply to your message.
  • To send the newsletter, if you subscribed. Every issue has an unsubscribe link.
  • To follow up about our services. You can tell us to stop at any time.
  • To understand how the site is used, keep it working, and stop spam and abuse.

4. Where it's stored and who sees it

Form submissions are saved as contact records in HubSpot, the customer database we use. We use AI tools to help organize leads and draft replies. A person at LeadVerdict reviews and sends every reply.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • The service providers above (HubSpot, Vercel, Google, Meta), to run the site and our business
  • Anyone the law requires us to share it with
  • A buyer or successor, if LeadVerdict is ever sold or merged
  • Anyone else you ask us to share it with

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while we're talking with you or working together, and for a reasonable time after so we have a record. Ask us to delete it and we will, unless the law requires us to keep it.

6. Your choices

Email leadverdict.co@gmail.com to:

  • See the information we have about you
  • Correct it
  • Delete it
  • Stop marketing emails or calls

We'll respond within 30 days.

7. Security

We use reputable providers and limit who can access your information. No website or database is perfectly secure, so we can't promise that nothing will ever go wrong.

8. Children

Our services are for businesses. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has sent us information, email us and we'll delete it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top.
